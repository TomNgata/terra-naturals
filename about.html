Our Heritage

Bridging African Botanicals & Global Cosmetic Standards

When Loise Nguru founded Terra Naturals in 2017, Kenyan beauty entrepreneurs faced two major hurdles: finding reliable, cosmetic-grade natural raw materials locally, and accessing formal, science-backed formulation training that met KEBS and international safety standards.

Today, from our studio along Kiambu Road in Nairobi, Terra Naturals serves hundreds of skincare makers, haircare founders, spas, and NGO community cooperatives across all 47 Kenyan counties—combining 79+ stocked raw materials with confidential R&D advisory and practical training.
