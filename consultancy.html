Advisory FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers on how our formulation sessions and regulatory guidance work.

Can consultations be done virtually if I am outside Nairobi?
Yes. Our 1.5-hour technical consultation (KES 4,500) is available both in-person at our Kiambu Road Nairobi studio and via video call for beauty entrepreneurs across Kenya and East Africa.
What is included when I commission a custom product formulation?
Custom formulation projects are quoted according to product complexity and typically cover ingredient research, bench sample prototyping, pH and basic stability checks, full percentage formula sheet, manufacturing method, and compliant INCI listing.
Do you help with KEBS certification preparation?
Yes. We guide you through Kenyan cosmetic labelling standards, INCI accuracy, preservation safety, workspace GMP layout, and documentation needed ahead of KEBS inspection and KRA registration.
