4 Rules for KEBS-Compliant Cosmetic Ingredient Labels

Preparing clean, internationally standardized labels builds retailer trust and speeds up KEBS certification in Kenya.

RULE 01

Descending Weight Order

List all ingredients present above 1% in strict descending order of percentage by weight in the finished formula (usually starting with Aqua for lotions).

RULE 02

Use Official INCI Names

Botanicals must use standardized Latin binomials (e.g., Butyrospermum Parkii Butter instead of simply writing "Shea Butter").

RULE 03

Expand Multi-Part Blends

Pre-blended preservatives and emulsifiers (like Geogard ECT or Olivem 1000) must have every constituent INCI component listed individually.

RULE 04

Batch Code & Shelf Life

Include batch/lot number, net weight/volume, manufacturer address in Kenya, usage precautions, and expiry or PAO symbol.
